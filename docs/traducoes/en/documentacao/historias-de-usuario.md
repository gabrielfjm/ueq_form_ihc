# User stories

**44 stories** state what each [persona](personas.md) wants to do with the UEQ Tool and how to know it worked. All 44 are delivered. The same list is in [historias-de-usuario.csv](historias-de-usuario.csv) and in the [requirements spreadsheet](planilha-requisitos.xlsx).

## How to read

Each story follows the format **As** (who), **I want** (what), **so that** (why), written from the persona's point of view. The **acceptance criteria** state what has to happen in the tool for the story to be considered done: each criterion can be checked on screen or in a test case.

A story is **delivered** when all the requirements it cites are delivered. The link between each requirement and the stories, use cases and tests is in the [traceability matrix](matriz-rastreabilidade.md).

## Researchers

### HU01

**Create an account and log in**

As **Marina**, researcher ([PE01](personas.md#pe01)), **I want** to create an account and log in without reading a manual, **so that** I can start setting up my study on the same day.

Acceptance criteria:

1. The password rules appear before I type.
2. If a field is wrong, a summary at the top says which one it is and takes me to it.
3. Log in and Create account open in a dialog over the home page, and Esc closes it and returns the focus.
4. After logging in, I go back to the page I had requested.

Requirements: [RF01](requisitos.md#rf01), [RF02](requisitos.md#rf02), [RF11](requisitos.md#rf11). Status: Delivered.

### HU02

**Learn how to use it on first access**

As **Marina**, researcher ([PE01](personas.md#pe01)), **I want** to be guided through the screens the first time and to have help on every page, **so that** I do not get stuck on a screen I do not understand.

Acceptance criteria:

1. On first access, a tour shows the actions on the real screens, and I move forward by performing the action.
2. I can skip or close the tour and review it later through the help.
3. Every researcher page has a (?) that explains the page itself.

Requirements: [RF09](requisitos.md#rf09), [RF10](requisitos.md#rf10). Status: Delivered.

### HU03

**Create a study with two versions**

As **Marina**, researcher ([PE01](personas.md#pe01)), **I want** to register the study with the two versions of the app, **so that** I can compare the current version with the new one in the same study.

Acceptance criteria:

1. I enter the title, product, objective, period and versions.
2. The product name suggests the name of the first version.
3. Errors appear in a summary with a link to each field, and nothing is lost.
4. The study is created as a draft, without accepting responses.

Requirements: [RF13](requisitos.md#rf13), [RF19](requisitos.md#rf19). Status: Delivered.

### HU04

**State who is responsible for the study**

As **Marina**, researcher ([PE01](personas.md#pe01)), **I want** to say in the consent form who I am, how to contact me, the risks and the benefits, **so that** I comply with research ethics and inspire confidence in those who answer.

Acceptance criteria:

1. Name, contact email and institution are filled in from my account and are required.
2. Risks and benefits come with a suggested text that I can change.
3. The ethics committee is optional and appears in the consent form when provided.
4. I see the preview of the participant's consent form next to the form.

Requirements: [RF13](requisitos.md#rf13), [RF25](requisitos.md#rf25). Status: Delivered.

### HU05

**Decide whether to collect demographic data**

As **Marina**, researcher ([PE01](personas.md#pe01)), **I want** to choose, for each study, whether the link asks for age group, gender, education level and familiarity, **so that** I collect only what my research needs.

Acceptance criteria:

1. Demographic data collection is turned off by default.
2. When I turn it on, the consent form says so and the estimated time increases.
3. The demographic data are encrypted in the database.

Requirements: [RF18](requisitos.md#rf18), [RNF13](requisitos.md#rnf13), [RNF14](requisitos.md#rnf14). Status: Delivered.

### HU08

**Understand the result without being a statistician**

As **Marina**, researcher ([PE01](personas.md#pe01)), **I want** to read the result with the interpretation written next to each number, **so that** I know whether the new version is better without becoming an expert.

Acceptance criteria:

1. A sentence at the top summarizes the result.
2. Each scale shows the mean, CI95, alpha, interpretation and benchmark range, in the chart and in a table.
3. The technical terms have a short explanation that opens right there.
4. With a low alpha or a small sample, a warning says what it means and what to do.
5. The chart remains readable without relying on color.

Requirements: [RF38](requisitos.md#rf38), [RF43](requisitos.md#rf43), [RF44](requisitos.md#rf44), [RF45](requisitos.md#rf45), [RF49](requisitos.md#rf49), [RNF07](requisitos.md#rnf07), [RNF08](requisitos.md#rnf08). Status: Delivered.

### HU10

**Compare the two versions**

As **Marina**, researcher ([PE01](personas.md#pe01)), **I want** to see the versions side by side in the same chart, **so that** I can say on which scales the new version came out ahead.

1. Each participant is assigned to the version with the fewest responses, so the groups stay similar.

3. The version and the mode are kept in the address: reloading or sending the link keeps the same view.

Requirements: [RF26](requisitos.md#rf26), [RF47](requisitos.md#rf47), [RF48](requisitos.md#rf48). Status: Delivered.

### HU12

**Remove careless responses from the analysis**

As **Marina**, researcher ([PE01](personas.md#pe01)), **I want** to see which responses look careless and remove them from the calculation, **so that** people who answered at random do not distort the result.

Acceptance criteria:

1. The tool flags as suspicious anyone with 3 or more inconsistent scales or more than 15 identical answers.
2. I see the reason and the range of each of the participant's scales.
3. I remove and restore them one by one, or all suspicious ones at once, without deleting anything.
4. The dashboard numbers then use only those who are in the analysis.

Requirements: [RF42](requisitos.md#rf42), [RF51](requisitos.md#rf51), [RF52](requisitos.md#rf52), [RF53](requisitos.md#rf53), [RF54](requisitos.md#rf54). Status: Delivered.
